# XPrism Code of Conduct

XPrism is built by people of every background, and we want everyone who
takes part to feel safe and welcome: contributors, users, reviewers,
people asking questions, and people who just read along.

## Our commitment

We welcome everyone, whatever their age, body, ability or disability,
ethnicity, nationality, caste, religion or lack of one, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, sexual or romantic
orientation, relationship structure, family situation, appearance,
language, education, income, or level of experience. LGBTQIAP+ people
belong here, and so does anyone using X11 for the first time.

We commit to making participation in XPrism free of harassment and
discrimination, and to treating each other with respect, also when we
disagree.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Respect people's names and pronouns. If you don't know them, use
  their username and "they".
- Review code, not people. Explain what is wrong and why, and how it
  could be better.
- Accept that others may decide differently, and accept correction
  gracefully. Admit and fix your own mistakes.
- Make room for others: newcomers, people writing in their second
  language, people who need more time.
- Keep private things private, including what others tell you in
  confidence and anyone's personal details.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or threats, in public or private.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning jokes and comments, including about any of
  the characteristics listed above.
- Deliberately misgendering someone or using a name they no longer use.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without their
  explicit permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of
  the above.

## Where this applies

In every XPrism space: the repositories and their issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions, chats, mailing lists, events, and anywhere you
represent the project, such as an official account or talk.

## Reporting

If something here happens to you or you see it happen, please tell the
maintainers privately: open a private report at
<https://github.com/XPrismOrg/xserver/security/advisories/new> and start
the title with **"Conduct:"**. Only the maintainers can read it. You can
also report content to GitHub itself with "Report content" on any
comment.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will not reveal who reported, and
a maintainer who is involved in a report takes no part in handling it.
We will reply as soon as we can, and tell you what we decided.

## What happens then

The maintainers decide what is fair for the situation, for example:

1. **A private note**: explaining what was harmful, with a request to
   change. An apology may be asked for.
2. **A warning**: with consequences for continuing, and possibly a pause
   in interacting with the people involved.
3. **A temporary ban**: from all project spaces for a set time.
4. **A permanent ban**: for a pattern of violations, or for one serious
   one such as harassment or threats.

Retaliating against someone for reporting is itself a violation.
